Cookie policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This page explains how 87px.co.uk uses them.

Cookies we use

None. This site doesn't set any cookies, and it doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels or similar tracking.

Other services

  • Google Fonts delivers our typefaces. It doesn't set cookies, but your browser does send your IP address to Google to fetch the fonts.
  • YouTube. The film tiles are plain links. YouTube may set its own cookies once you're on its site, under its own policy.
  • Other sites we link to, such as our clients' and partners' websites, have their own cookie policies.

Cookie settings

There's nothing to switch on or off. Because this site sets no cookies, there are no optional cookies to accept or refuse. If that ever changes, we'll ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookie, and you'll be able to change your choice here.

You can also block or delete cookies for any website in your browser's settings.
